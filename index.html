About

I am a Scientific Machine Learning engineer with more than five years of research in physics-informed deep learning, neural operators, and lattice Boltzmann / CFD modelling of multiphase and microscale flows.

I currently build production-grade SciML models and research software at Zenteiq AI Innovation, including thermal surrogate models delivered to two industry clients. My work covers the full pipeline: model design, GPU training, hyperparameter optimisation and deployment.

I have authored 28 research outputs: 9 journal articles (including one accepted in Langmuir), 4 under review, 12 conference papers (including AAAI-26 and the APS March Meeting) and 3 book chapters.

Doctoral Researcher: Scientific Machine Learning & Microfluidics

2019 – 2026
Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, West Bengal
  • Developed Droplet-LNO, a physics-informed Laplace Neural Operator that predicts droplet spreading dynamics on complex surfaces.
  • Coupled PINNs with the lattice Boltzmann method to predict unsteady flow in fractal-rough microchannels and droplet wetting on rough surfaces.
  • Built an Attention U-Net–LBM surrogate for real-time flow prediction in patterned microchannels, plus image-analysis-driven physics-guided deep learning models for irregular-shaped channels.
  • Created sequence models (LSTM, LST-Nets) for droplet contact-angle and spreading prediction, and ML classifiers for wetting-state demarcation.
  • Wrote in-house pseudo-potential multiphase LBM solvers (C, Python, MATLAB) to generate high-fidelity training data; trained models on GPU and HPC clusters.

Supervisors: Prof. Suman Chakraborty (Mechanical Engineering) and Prof. Partha P. Chakrabarti (Computer Science & Engineering).

Research highlights

Neural operator

Droplet-LNO

A physics-informed Laplace Neural Operator that predicts droplet spreading dynamics on complex surfaces.

PINN + LBM

Lattice-Boltzmann-driven PINNs

Physics-informed networks coupled with the lattice Boltzmann method to learn droplet wetting on rough surfaces and unsteady flow in fractal-rough microchannels.

Real-time surrogate

Attention U-Net–LBM

A fast surrogate for flow prediction in patterned microchannels, replacing repeated solver runs with near-instant inference.

Physics-guided DL

Irregular-shaped microchannels

Image-analysis-inspired, physics-guided deep learning for predicting fluid flow in channels with irregular geometry.

Sequence models

LSTM and LST-Nets for wetting

Sequence models for droplet contact-angle and spreading prediction, with ML classifiers that demarcate wetting states of textured surfaces.
